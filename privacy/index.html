PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: November 2026 • Effective for all guests of Elafo Hair

Zero Surveillance Policy

1. Our Privacy Philosophy

At Elafo Hair ("we", "our", or "studio"), your trust is paramount. We believe privacy is an essential courtesy to our clients. We do not engage in surveillance advertising, we do not track you across other websites, and we do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal information to data brokers or third parties under any circumstances.

This website is built with a minimal digital footprint: static HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript without external tracking trackers, ad networks, or third-party behavioral scripts.

100% On-Device Processing

2. Virtual Hairstyle Try-On Privacy

Our interactive Virtual Hairstyle Try-On feature operates entirely on your personal device using native HTML5 <canvas> technology:

  • No Server Upload: When you select or snap a photo, it is loaded directly into your local browser's memory. The image is never transmitted over the Internet or saved on any server.
  • No Facial Recognition Database: We do not extract biometric facial geometry, facial prints, or identity markers. The overlay positioning is controlled manually by you.
  • Zero Remote Storage: Once you close or reload the try-on tool, your photo is instantly purged from temporary browser memory.
  • Exporting: If you choose to export your composite photo, the PNG file is generated locally by your browser for you to download or share.
Direct Client Communication

3. Appointment Inquiries & Contact Data

When you submit a consultation request through our on-site booking wizard:

  • The contact details you provide (name, phone number, styling notes) are temporarily held in your browser's local sessionStorage solely to assemble your appointment summary.
  • Transmissions are handed directly to your device's native communication apps (SMS text via sms:, direct phone call via tel:, or email via mailto:), or our secure booking platform.
  • We retain your contact details solely to confirm appointment dates, send service reminders, and coordinate hairstyle consultations.
No Tracking Cookies

4. Cookies, Analytics & Offline Storage

We do not use advertising cookies, profiling cookies, or cross-site tracking pixels:

  • Zero Tracking Cookies: No third-party ad networks or data harvesting cookies are loaded on our pages.
  • Progressive Web App (Service Worker): To ensure reliable performance and offline availability, our Service Worker caches static site files (fonts, logos, CSS, and service menu) in your browser's local storage. This data contains no personal information and can be cleared at any time via your browser settings.

5. Children's Privacy Protection

While we proudly braid hair for young children with parent or guardian accompaniment, our website is intended for general audiences and adults seeking hair services. We do not knowingly collect personal contact information from children under the age of 13.
