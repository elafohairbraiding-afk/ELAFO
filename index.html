Client Respect & Peace Of Mind

DEPOSIT & STUDIO POLICIES

Clear, transparent standards to ensure seamless artistry and dedicated attention for every guest.

A non-refundable $50 deposit is required to confirm your reserved appointment slot. This deposit is 100% credited toward your final hairstyle balance on appointment day. Zero hidden chair surcharges.

Please arrive promptly. We provide a complimentary 15-minute grace window. Beyond 20 minutes without prior text notice, appointments may need to be rescheduled to protect the dedicated hours of other scheduled guests.

Please arrive with clean, product-free, detangled hair gently blown out from roots to tips. If you prefer to relax while we handle preparation, simply add our $25 Botanical Shampoo & Steam Prep add-on when booking.

Children receiving braiding services are warmly welcomed with one accompanying parent. For safety reasons regarding high-heat thermal tools and hot-water dipping stations, non-serviced young children are not permitted in the styling studio.

We accept Cash, Zelle, Apple Pay, Debit/Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex), and Venmo at the studio upon appointment completion. Itemized receipts are sent via SMS or email immediately.

With at least 48 hours notice prior to your appointment time, your $50 deposit may be transferred once to any open slot within 30 days without penalty.
