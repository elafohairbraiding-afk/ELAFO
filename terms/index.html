TERMS OF SERVICE & POLICIES

To ensure seamless, stress-free appointments and undivided artisan attention for every guest.

1. Appointment Deposit Requirement

A non-refundable $50 deposit is required to confirm every reserved appointment slot.

Braiding sessions require extensive blocks of dedicated time. To protect our artisans' schedule and reserve your specific styling window, a $50 deposit is collected upon booking.

  • Credit toward service: Your entire $50 deposit is directly credited to your final balance on the day of your appointment.
  • Non-refundable: Deposits are non-refundable in the event of client cancellation or no-show.
  • Rescheduling: With at least 48 hours notice, your deposit may be transferred once to a new appointment date within 30 days.

2. Punctuality & 15-Minute Grace Period

We honor a 15-minute grace period. Please notify us promptly if you encounter travel delays.

Because intricate styles require uninterrupted focus, punctuality ensures every client receives the relaxed, unhurried care they deserve:

  • 0–15 Minutes: Please notify us via text message. Your service will proceed as scheduled.
  • Beyond 20 Minutes: If you are more than 20 minutes late without confirmation, your appointment may need to be shortened (e.g. adjusted length/size) or rescheduled to avoid overlapping with subsequent clients.
  • No-Shows: Clients who fail to arrive without prior notification forfeit their deposit and may be restricted from reserving future peak weekend slots.

3. Hair Preparation & Cleanliness

To achieve crisp, razor-sharp partings and maximize the longevity of your braids, please arrive with your hair properly prepped:

  • Hair must be thoroughly washed, free of heavy grease, heavy oils, or leave-in build-up.
  • Hair must be completely detangled and gently blown out smooth from roots to tips.
  • Studio Wash Add-On: If you prefer to have your hair washed and blown out at our studio, please select the Shampoo & Scalp Prep ($25) add-on when booking so appropriate time is allocated.
  • If you have extreme scalp irritation, open wounds, or severe medical scalp conditions, please consult a physician prior to booking. We reserve the right to decline service if tension might compromise your scalp health.

4. Guests & Children Studio Policy

Our salon is a sanctuary designed for calm, focused craftsmanship and relaxation:

  • Children Receiving Services: Young guests receiving hair braiding are warmly welcomed. One parent or guardian is invited to accompany them throughout the appointment.
  • Non-Serviced Children: For liability and safety reasons (sharp styling tools, hot water dipping stations, high-heat tools), young children who are not receiving services are not permitted in the styling studio.
  • Additional Guests: Due to comfortable seating capacity, please limit extra visitors to one companion if necessary.

5. Transparent Pricing & Payment

We pride ourselves on 100% upfront pricing with zero unexpected chair fees:

  • Standard prices shown on our menu reflect Mid-back length and natural 1B/2/4 extensions.
  • Waist length (+$30), Butt length (+$50), and Thigh length (+$80) extensions require additional time and materials as detailed in our pricing calculator.
  • Specialty curl bundles (human hair or synthetic blend) for Bohemian styles are priced transparently as outlined during consultation.
  • Accepted Payment Methods: Cash, Zelle, Apple Pay, Major Debit & Credit Cards, and Venmo.

6. Satisfaction Guarantee & Aftercare

Before stepping out of our chair, we provide a 360-degree mirror review of your hairline, perimeter tension, and length. If any braid feels too tight or requires adjustment, our artisans will immediately adjust it on the spot.

We provide complimentary aftercare guidance (satin bonnet usage, tea tree scalp misting) to help your crown maintain its pristine beauty for 8 to 10 weeks.
